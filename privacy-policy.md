Privacy Policy for Coop Track

This extension collects job posting information from the UTSC Co-op portal 
and writes it to your personal Google Sheet. 

- We do not collect or store your data on any external server
- We do not share your data with any third parties
- All data is written directly to your own Google Drive
- The only data stored locally is your Google Sheet ID, used to append 
  future applications to the same sheet
